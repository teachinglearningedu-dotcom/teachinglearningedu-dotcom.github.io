Individual Development

Use this site to choose, try and refine one change to your classroom practice.

Individual Development CPD gives you protected time to improve one specific aspect of your classroom practice. You choose a focus from your PGD, try a small change, use peer observation to see what happens and refine your approach across the year.

This site is designed to help at every stage. Each strategy page includes an example goal, what a peer observer could look for and a quick way to check whether the change is helping pupils.

The improvement cycle

  1. Identify. Name a pupil learning problem you can see in your classroom, and what pupils should do differently.
  2. Explore. Use the pupil needs pages or the strategy library to find evidence-informed options.
  3. Plan. Choose one small, precise change. Write it as I will … so that students …
  4. Implement. Practise the change in normal lessons until it feels routine.
  5. Observe. A colleague gathers focused evidence on your change and how pupils respond.
  6. Reflect. Look at what pupils did differently. Separate how well you implemented the change from its effect on pupils.
  7. Adapt. Refine the change, embed it, or try something different in the next cycle.

Keep it manageable

  • One change at a time. A precise, small change you practise often beats several new ideas at once.
  • Use the look-fors on each strategy page instead of writing your own observation form.
  • Pupil work, mini-whiteboard responses and short checks are enough evidence. You do not need a formal research project.
