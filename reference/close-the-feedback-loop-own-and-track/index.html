Close the feedback loop: Own and Track

Ensuring students record the accurate answer, understand why it is accurate and visibly amend, annotate, redraft or re-practise their own work.

What it is: Ensuring students record the accurate answer, understand why it is accurate and visibly amend, annotate, redraft or re-practise their own work.

Why it helps: Feedback that is heard but not acted upon rarely changes learning. Visible correction and a fresh attempt make the response accountable and allow the teacher to verify impact.

Use it when: after verbal, written, peer or whole-class feedback and after any reteaching episode.

Implementation sequence

State the exact action students must take and where it should appear.

Model the correction or success feature if needed.

Give protected time for every student to amend or retry.

Ask students to explain the change or the rule behind it.

Scan or sample the corrected work.

Use a related task later to test durable improvement.

DODON'T
Make the action concrete, specific and visible. Get meta about both correct and incorrect answers. Praise changed thinking and successful self-correction. Move on after giving the answer without requiring a record. Accept a tick or copied model as proof of understanding. Reteach without rechecking.

Check for impact

Every student leaves with an accurate record and can explain the change.

The improved method transfers to a later task.

Sources informing this profile: StepFinder/TLAC: Own and Track; Tips for Teachers: Responsive Teaching.
