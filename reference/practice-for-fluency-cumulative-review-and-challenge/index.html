Practice for fluency, cumulative review and challenge

Sequencing repeated, varied and spaced opportunities so accurate knowledge or processes become increasingly fluent and can be applied in more complex contexts.

What it is: Sequencing repeated, varied and spaced opportunities so accurate knowledge or processes become increasingly fluent and can be applied in more complex contexts.

Why it helps: Fluency reduces the working-memory cost of component knowledge. Spacing and varied practice then strengthen retention and flexible use.

Use it when: knowledge and procedures students must retrieve or execute automatically as part of more complex performance.

Implementation sequence

Identify the component knowledge that should become fluent.

Begin with blocked or closely matched practice to establish the method.

Provide immediate correction and maintain productive success.

Increase quantity, spacing, variation and complexity gradually.

Mix in prior learning once students can discriminate which method applies.

Track performance across time, not only within one lesson.

DODON'T
Use personal bests or carefully framed competition where motivating. Schedule cumulative retrieval after initial success. Retain explanation and reasoning alongside speed. Push speed before accuracy and understanding. Use random interleaving before students know the component methods. Repeat identical items so mechanically that students stop thinking.

Check for impact

Accuracy remains high as delay and variation increase.

Students select and execute the method with less working-memory demand.

Sources informing this profile: StepFinder: Ensure Consolidation, Retrieval Quizzes and Increase the Challenge; DistilED: Retrieval and Independent Practice.
