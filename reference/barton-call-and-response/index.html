Core idea

Call and Response is a brief whole-class verbal response. It is efficient because it requires no equipment and can make everyone rehearse a key fact, term, phrase or routine. In the project summary, its value was greatest when the response was short, unambiguous and worth rehearsing.

Applications

  • Vocabulary pronunciation and definition stems.
  • Rapid retrieval of short facts or relationships.
  • Checks for listening during an explanation.
  • Knowledge-organiser self-quizzing and rehearsal.
  • Replacing unnecessary note-taking when the desired learning is accurate oral recall.

EFR and thinking time

For a response that requires thinking rather than simple repetition, use an Everybody-First-Respond / whole-class structure with at least a few seconds of thinking time. Choral response should not become an excuse to remove thinking.

Do / avoid

DoAvoid
Use for concise, common responses.Using choral response for ambiguous or lengthy answers.
Model the exact quality of response expected.Letting confident pupils shout first and cue everyone else.
Protect thinking time before a more demanding choral answer.Mistaking a loud chorus for individual understanding.
Repeat until pronunciation or phrasing is precise where precision matters.Using it as the only checking method for complex ideas.
Use it as one participation method within a wider sequence.

Explicit link to the BSCS model

  • Drive thinking for all: All pupils can rehearse a concise response simultaneously.
  • Explain: Supports checks for listening and vocabulary rehearsal without long interruptions.
  • Climate and culture: A predictable response routine can improve attention and collective participation.
  • Check and adapt: Useful as a listening check, but not sufficient by itself for diagnosing individual misconceptions.
