Plan the means of participation / Double plan

Planning both teacher actions and the cognitive actions students will take: thinking, retrieving, writing, discussing, explaining and revising.

What it is: Planning both teacher actions and the cognitive actions students will take: thinking, retrieving, writing, discussing, explaining and revising.

Why it helps: Teacher activity can conceal student passivity. A participation plan distributes opportunities to think and makes the intended learning behaviour observable without mistaking compliance for learning.

Use it when: any explanation, questioning sequence, discussion or feedback episode in which only a small number of students might otherwise respond.

Implementation sequence

For each lesson phase, write what students will think about and how they will respond.

Choose the response mode to match the knowledge: individual writing, whole-class response, paired rehearsal or individual explanation.

Front-load the means of participation before asking the question.

Plan movement from Everyone to Together to Anyone, retaining individual accountability.

Identify students who may need pre-call, writing time, vocabulary or an adjusted access route.

DODON'T
Protect private thinking time before public response. Use whole-class methods to sample everyone at pivotal points. Plan follow-up prompts that deepen rather than merely repeat answers. Write only what the teacher will say or show. Use hands-up as the default sampling method. Add paired talk without deciding what students should produce afterwards.

Check for impact

Across a lesson, every student repeatedly says, writes or does something connected to the content.

The teacher can explain why each participation method was selected.

Sources informing this profile: BSCS Policy; StepFinder; DistilED: Everyone, Together, Anyone; Tips for Teachers: Cold Call and Turn and Talk.
