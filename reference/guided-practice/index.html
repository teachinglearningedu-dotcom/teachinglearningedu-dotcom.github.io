Guided practice

Short supported rehearsal in which the teacher and students co-work examples, make responses visible and correct errors before independent release.

What it is: Short supported rehearsal in which the teacher and students co-work examples, make responses visible and correct errors before independent release.

Why it helps: Guided practice bridges explanation and independence. Immediate feedback prevents misconceptions from hardening and builds the high success needed for confident practice.

Use it when: immediately after modelling each small step and before students attempt the complete task independently.

Implementation sequence

Frame the task as rehearsal and focus on one taught step.

Model the first item, then elicit the next steps from all students.

Use mini-whiteboards, cold call, pair explanation or completion problems.

Circulate and correct misconceptions immediately.

Fade prompts as accuracy improves and check for roughly 80% success.

Release to a closely matched independent task.

DODON'T
Keep practice short, cumulative and visibly checked. Ask students to explain why the step works. Repeat a stage when response evidence is insecure. Let only a few volunteers practise aloud. Introduce new task demands during the readiness check. Rush to independent work to protect the planned schedule.

Check for impact

Most students succeed on a similar item without the teacher supplying each step.

The class can explain the process before release.

Sources informing this profile: DistilED: Guide Student Practice; StepFinder: Plan, Run and Monitor Independent Practice.
