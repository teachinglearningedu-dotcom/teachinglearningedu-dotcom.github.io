Purpose

The Do Now is a consistent opening routine designed principally for spaced retrieval and a purposeful start. In the project summary, it is not primarily a prerequisite check for the new lesson; prerequisite checking is a separate responsive-teaching decision point when needed.

Design

  • Use a consistent four-question structure, with an optional challenge question.
  • Aim for roughly three out of four correct on average: enough success to motivate, but enough difficulty to make retrieval worthwhile.
  • Order the questions from more accessible to more challenging where useful.
  • Prefer questions with a clear, single answer when the aim is rapid checking.
  • Keep the task short enough to protect lesson time: the project summary used roughly five minutes of work plus approximately five minutes of review as a useful benchmark.

Routine

  • 1. Have the task visible before pupils enter so no teacher explanation is needed to start.
  • 2. Use a visible timer and a silent, independent start.
  • 3. Complete two circulation laps where practical: one to secure the routine / register and one to gather information about learning.
  • 4. Latecomers join the task rather than interrupting the routine.
  • 5. If focus is poor, release one question at a time rather than abandoning the routine.

Checking and responsive review

The review should be proportionate to what pupils actually know. Book-to-Board MWBs, all-MWB responses, choral response, cold call or peer checking can be selected according to the question type. If almost everyone is correct, move quickly. If many are incorrect, model or re-teach the relevant point and then check again. Avoid giving hints before the check if those hints make the evidence unreliable.

Do / avoid

DoAvoid
Make the task immediately accessible and routine.Using the Do Now as generic busywork.
Use spaced retrieval rather than only yesterday’s learning.Turning every Do Now into prerequisite checking for today’s lesson.
Collect whole-class evidence efficiently.Revealing clues that invalidate the retrieval check.
Review responsively rather than spending equal time on every question.Lengthy whole-class review of items almost everyone has correct.
Engineer early success while preserving worthwhile difficulty.Starting with teacher talk before pupils have begun.

Explicit link to the BSCS model

  • Do Now: Direct enactment of the BSCS opening phase: immediate start, retrieval, early success and activation of prior learning.
  • Climate and culture: The predictable silent entry routine reduces transition time and secures purposeful behaviour.
  • Check and adapt: The review can generate evidence that leads to a quick model, targeted follow-up or re-teach.
  • Lesson preparation: Question selection should be deliberately spaced across the curriculum rather than improvised lesson by lesson.
