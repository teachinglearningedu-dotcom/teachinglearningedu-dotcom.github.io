BSCS agreed practice

Secure learning through practice

Move from guided rehearsal to confident independent performance.

Students engage in purposeful independent practice to develop fluency and understanding. Tasks are appropriately scaffolded, and teachers actively monitor learning. Students focus on thinking deeply about the content, without unnecessary distraction from task mechanics or resources.

Techniques in the BSCS model

We do; You do; High success rate; Scaffolding; Active observation

Professional judgement

Select and sequence techniques in response to the curriculum content and the pupils. The model may repeat within one lesson or span several lessons. These techniques are not a checklist to use all at once.

Source and status

BSCS Teaching and Learning Policy, June 2026, model of highly effective teaching.
