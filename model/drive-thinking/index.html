BSCS agreed practice

Drive Thinking for All

Give every pupil time to think, contribute and explain their reasoning.

Lessons are designed so that students are thinking - not simply completing tasks, which is a poor proxy for learning. Teachers carefully sequence participation to ensure all students think deeply about key content. Students are given opportunities to think independently, refine ideas collaboratively, and remain accountable for their own responses. High participation ratios ensure that all students are engaged, not just a few. This promotes equitable participation, accountability, and the development of oracy.

Techniques in the BSCS model

Silent Solo (Everybody Writes); Show Me Boards (MWBs); Cold Call; Call and Response; Turn and Talk; Wait Time; Volunteers; No Opt Out; Right is Right; Stretch It; Process questions

Professional judgement

Select and sequence techniques in response to the curriculum content and the pupils. The model may repeat within one lesson or span several lessons. These techniques are not a checklist to use all at once.
