Feedback to improve

Make feedback precise and give pupils time to act on it.

Teachers review and respond to student work, addressing misconceptions, reinforcing success, and making thinking visible to improve learning. Students improve their work.

Techniques in the BSCS model

Show call; Thinking aloud; Whole-class feedback; Structured peer assessments

Professional judgement

Select and sequence techniques in response to the curriculum content and the pupils. The model may repeat within one lesson or span several lessons. These techniques are not a checklist to use all at once.

Source and status

BSCS Teaching and Learning Policy, June 2026, model of highly effective teaching.
