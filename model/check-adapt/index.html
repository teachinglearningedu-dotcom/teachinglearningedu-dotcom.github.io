BSCS agreed practice

Check & Adapt

Gather useful evidence, choose a response and check again.

Teachers gather whole-class feedback throughout the lesson to assess understanding and make responsive adaptations to instruction, ensuring all students keep up with the learning

Techniques in the BSCS model

Check: Active listening during explanations (e.g. call and response); Show Me Boards (MWBs); Turn and Talk followed by Cold call; Hinge questions Adapt: Re-explaining; Flexible groupings; Scaffolding; Guided practice extension; Stretch it; increasing complexity; peer explanation

Professional judgement

Select and sequence techniques in response to the curriculum content and the pupils. The model may repeat within one lesson or span several lessons. These techniques are not a checklist to use all at once.

Source and status

BSCS Teaching and Learning Policy, June 2026, model of highly effective teaching.
