BSCS agreed practice

Explain

Make new knowledge clear through small steps and expert modelling.

New content is delivered through explicit instruction using clear, concise language, modelling, and carefully sequenced steps. Teaching connects to prior knowledge and manages cognitive load. Key vocabulary is explicitly highlighted and recorded. Teachers ensure full attention and active listening

Techniques in the BSCS model

Linking to prior knowledge; Chunking; Dual coding; Reduce redundancy; Thinking aloud; Chunking; Active listening; “I do”; Explicit vocabulary instruction

Professional judgement

Select and sequence techniques in response to the curriculum content and the pupils. The model may repeat within one lesson or span several lessons. These techniques are not a checklist to use all at once.

Source and status

BSCS Teaching and Learning Policy, June 2026, model of highly effective teaching.
