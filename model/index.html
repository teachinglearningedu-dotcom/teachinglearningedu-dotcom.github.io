BSCS agreed practice

Our model of highly effective teaching

Plan deliberately, protect attention and make thinking visible. The model supports professional judgement across subjects and phases.

01

Lesson preparation

Plan from the learning goal, anticipate barriers and decide how pupils will think.

02

Climate & Culture

Build warm relationships and consistent routines that protect attention.

03

Drive Thinking for All

Give every pupil time to think, contribute and explain their reasoning.

04

Check & Adapt

Gather useful evidence, choose a response and check again.

05

Do Now

Start calmly with independent retrieval and purposeful review.

06

Explain

Make new knowledge clear through small steps and expert modelling.

07

Secure learning through practice

Move from guided rehearsal to confident independent performance.

08

Feedback to improve

Make feedback precise and give pupils time to act on it.
