BSCS agreed routine

Entry and Do Now

  • 1. Before students enter the classroom, the teacher ensures that the Do Now is visible and ready. Students use calm waiting if the teacher is not ready (calm waiting: quietly lined up in a single file and with their shoulders against the wall)

Required resources should be ready where possible, for example books, sheets, MWBs, equipment, knowledge organisers or Do Now booklets

If the teacher is not yet ready to receive students, students wait calmly until teacher at Threshold

  • 2. Teacher moves to threshold as soon as possible and starts the timer for the Do Now

The teacher stands at the threshold as soon as possible, positioned so they can:

greet students warmly;

monitor the corridor and support other staff, in particular students lining up for nomadic teachers

monitor their classroom/ direct students quickly into learning

Between 3 and a maximum of 5 minutes to be set on the timer.

The timer starts from the point at which the member of staff is ready at the threshold and the first student enters, not from the point when every student is seated. This creates urgency and protects the Do Now as the first phase of learning

  • 3. Teacher starts flowing entry, remaining at Threshold. Teacher Meets and Greets and checks readiness during entry

If the teacher is ready at the threshold as students arrive, students should not wait to form a full line. The teacher uses a flowing entry to get students out of the corridor quickly and into learning, but they control the pace to ensure the classroom is calm.

e.g. “Good morning. In silence go straight in, collect your books, get your equipment out and begin the Do Now in silence.”

Then meet and greet each student individually:

e.g. “Good morning, [student name], Good morning, [student name]”

The teacher checks readiness as students enter e.g. calmness, uniform

Any corrections should be brief, precise and low-emotion. e.g. “Thank you. Enter calmly, straight to your seat.”

  • 4. Once students enter, they collect equipment using the agreed team system and begin Do Now immediately in silence

Students enter silently, go directly to their set seat as per seating plan, collect books/ take out equipment (curriculum team agreed routine) and begin the Do Now in silence.

The expectation once inside and during this phase is:

no wandering;

no conversation;

no waiting passively;

no avoidable delay.

  • 5. Teacher to positively narrate compliance and, if required, use anonymous correction

e.g. “Thank you to the first three in: seated, books open, already started and working in silence”

This should be from the threshold until a critical mass of students arrive (as it further supports corridor behaviour) If required use anonymous correction: “I am still waiting for two to start the Do Now”

  • 6. If further required, correct precisely where needed

If a student does not follow the Do Now routine, give a clear what-to-do instruction using the by Name , neutrally describe the behaviour/ direct them to the expected behaviour, and make the consequence clear.

e.g. “Name, you are talking. You need to complete the Do Now silently so that you and others can focus. I am giving you a warning. Thank you”

  • 7. Teacher to take the register with class in silence
  • 8. At the signal of the end of the timer, teachers use the whole school attention signal

When the timer ends, the teacher uses the school signal for attention (see signal section)

Instruction begins only once attention is fully secured.

  • 9. Check Do Now answers and start next learning phase

Why?

To ensure every lesson starts calmly, predictably and purposefully, reducing corridor congestion and behaviour issues, reducing cognitive load and maximising learning time.

Entry routine

Post-16 expectations

Start lessons with timed Do Now, meet and greet and the teacher is to remain on Threshold in order to support corridor behaviour
