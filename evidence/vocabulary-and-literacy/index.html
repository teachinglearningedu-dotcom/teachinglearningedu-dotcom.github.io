Evidence summary

Disciplinary literacy and explicit vocabulary instruction

BSCS COMPONENT: Lesson preparation; Explain; Drive thinking for all; Secure learning through practice; Feedback to improve

BSCS COMPONENT: Lesson preparation; Explain; Drive thinking for all; Secure learning through practice; Feedback to improve

EVIDENCE CLASSIFICATION: EEF guidance-based evidence, reinforced by the oral-language and reading-comprehension Toolkit strands.

STUDENT PROBLEM: Students do not understand subject vocabulary, misread command words, write generic rather than disciplinary responses, or cannot communicate how experts in the subject reason.

EEF SUMMARY: EEF's secondary literacy guidance treats literacy as subject specific as well as general. Every subject should explicitly teach how experts read, write, speak and use vocabulary in that discipline.

DO

Select a manageable set of essential words and teach meaning, pronunciation, morphology and use.

Model how experts read questions, sources, explanations and representations in the subject.

Use examples and non-examples and require active oral and written use.

Teach writing processes, sentence relationships and command-word decisions explicitly.

Diagnose whether the barrier is vocabulary, decoding, fluency, syntax, background knowledge or comprehension.

DON'T

Treat literacy as the sole responsibility of English teachers.

Display long keyword lists without explicit teaching and retrieval.

Assume copying a definition creates usable vocabulary knowledge.

Reduce disciplinary language so far that pupils cannot access ambitious subject thinking.

Apply the same generic reading or writing strategy in every subject without adaptation.

AGENT USE: Use for short answers, imprecise explanations, weak comprehension and subject vocabulary. Connect teacher modelling and thinking aloud to metacognition as well as literacy guidance.

Primary EEF sources: Guidance: Improving Literacy in Secondary Schools; Toolkit: Oral language interventions; Toolkit: Reading comprehension strategies
