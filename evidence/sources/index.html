Source hierarchy

Evidence, expectations and implementation

Know what supports the advice, and how far the claim can travel.

How authority works

Research evidence anchors claims about broad principles and evidence strength. BSCS policy defines school expectations. Evidence-informed professional learning explains mechanisms. Practitioner summaries provide implementation detail. These roles should remain distinct.

Source layerUse it forKeep in mind
EEF research and guidanceMechanisms, evidence strength and implementation principlesA broad intervention estimate is not an effect size for an individual classroom routine.
BSCS policy and agreed routinesSchool expectations and shared languageSchool agreement is not itself a research finding.
School summaries of professional learningTranslation into classroom practiceA compilation is not an original publisher document.
Practitioner strategy profilesScripts, sequences and common pitfallsUse professional judgement and check pupil learning.

What has been converted

Fifteen supplied files were extracted into structured Markdown and HTML, including duplicate staff-routines presentations and identical June policy copies. The public website selects the agreed routines, model descriptions, 50 school-compiled profiles, 16 Craig Barton project-summary entries, evidence pages and adviser guidance.

Public and restricted content

The website does not host the original subscription resources, the full StepFinder micro-strategy catalogue, or the detailed Ambition extraction. The full text conversion pack is retained separately for the owner. Adaptive teaching is represented publicly through a brief original synthesis and the relevant practical profiles.

Dates and updates

Conversion date: 12 October 2026. School model: supplied June 2026 policy. Strategy compilations: August 2026. Evidence source checking: 18 August 2026, as recorded in the supplied evidence document. No live refresh of research values is implied.

Coverage and limitations

Page search covers the published HTML content. Copilot uses web retrieval and may not find every page. Publishing and submitting a sitemap support discoverability, but do not guarantee indexing or complete retrieval. The adviser should cite the page it actually retrieved and acknowledge missing support.
