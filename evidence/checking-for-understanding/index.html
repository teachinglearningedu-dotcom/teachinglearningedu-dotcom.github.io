Evidence summary

Checking for understanding and responsive adaptation

BSCS COMPONENT: Check and Adapt; Drive thinking for all; Explain; Secure learning through practice

BSCS COMPONENT: Check and Adapt; Drive thinking for all; Explain; Secure learning through practice

EVIDENCE CLASSIFICATION: EEF guidance-based synthesis drawing on feedback evidence and the 2026 Check. Adapt. practitioner tool. No single effect-size estimate applies to cold call, mini-whiteboards or hinge questions.

STUDENT PROBLEM: Teachers receive unrepresentative or unreliable evidence, discover misconceptions too late, continue despite widespread misunderstanding, or check understanding without changing what happens next.

EEF SUMMARY: Effective checking is anchored to the intended learning, gathers valid evidence from all or nearly all pupils, makes error safe, and triggers a proportionate next move: pause and fix, adapt support, or extend and support.

DO

Plan what understanding the check must reveal and what responses would mean.

Use simultaneous or representative response methods and protect independent thinking.

Interpret the response pattern rather than relying on one confident answer.

Select a response: reteach differently, provide precise support, continue with monitoring, or deepen challenge.

Use a fresh related check after adaptation.

DON'T

Use 'Any questions?' or 'Does that make sense?' as the main evidence of understanding.

Rely on volunteers as a representative sample of the class.

Give hints that make a correct response impossible to interpret.

Collect responses and move on without acting on the information.

Treat a checking technique as evidence-based in isolation from its purpose and response.

AGENT USE: MWBs, ABCD cards, hinge questions, Turn and Talk followed by Cold Call and brief written responses are BSCS techniques aligned with EEF guidance. Explain the quality conditions and next action; do not attach a Toolkit effect size to the technique.

Primary EEF sources: EEF: Check. Adapt.; Guidance: Teacher Feedback to Improve Pupil Learning; EEF: Checking for understanding
