Evidence summary

Homework

ALIASES: home learning; retrieval homework; independent consolidation; revision practice

BSCS COMPONENT: Lesson preparation; Do Now; Secure learning through practice; Feedback to improve

EEF EVIDENCE HEADLINE: +5 months average impact; very low cost; low evidence security; 43 studies; Toolkit review updated August 2021.

EVIDENCE STATUS: Direct EEF Toolkit evidence. Secondary studies show greater average impact than primary studies, but evidence security is low.

STUDENT PROBLEM: Students do not retain knowledge between lessons, lack independent practice, complete homework mechanically, or experience unequal access to resources and support.

MECHANISM: Well-designed homework adds purposeful independent practice, consolidation, retrieval or preparation that is connected to classroom learning and followed up through feedback or accountability.

KEY EEF FINDINGS

Quality appears more important than quantity, and impact may diminish as time spent increases.

Homework linked closely to classroom learning and followed by feedback tends to show stronger effects.

Secondary studies show a higher average impact than primary studies.

Access to quiet space, devices, materials and support can create barriers and widen disadvantage gaps.

DO

Set a clear learning purpose linked to taught curriculum content.

Prioritise consolidation, practice and retrieval over substantial untaught content.

Design for reasonable time, clarity and independent completion.

Follow up visibly through checking, responsive review, feedback or later use.

Anticipate access barriers and provide practical alternatives or supported completion.

DON'T

Use homework as a punishment or as unbounded extra work.

Assume more time or more questions automatically creates more learning.

Set substantial new learning without prior instruction and support.

Ignore whether pupils had equitable access to devices, space or resources.

Set work that is never checked, used or responded to.

AGENT USE: Use for problems involving consolidation, retention and independent study outside lessons. Keep retrieval Do Nows separate: the EEF homework estimate does not apply to an in-class opening routine.
